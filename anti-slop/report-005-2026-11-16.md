## Follow-up: progress dialog unwired (reverts report-004)

User decision: copy/move must not open a modal dialog, at any size. With several tabs open, each running its own transfer, a modal blocks switching tabs. `_executeTransfer` again only queues the job and sets the status line. Progress, pause, cancel and name conflicts stay in the non-blocking transfer bar. `_showTransferProgress` was removed. `FileExplorerProgressDialog` stays in the codebase with its tests, but nothing in the app opens it now.

Verification after this change: analyze shows no errors, and `flutter test` passes 257 tests.
