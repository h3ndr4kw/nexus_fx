## Numbered Findings List

### [HIGH] Hard Gate (Honesty, Accessibility, & Function)

- **[01] R-02 (Copywriting: Em Dash)**  
  `lib/screens/nexus_screen_view.dart:166` contains forbidden em dash (`—`) in banner text `'PRODUCTION ENVIRONMENT — EXECUTE COMMANDS WITH CAUTION'`; replace with colon or comma.

- **[02] R-35 & R-36 (Verification & No Fabricated Claims)**  
  `README.md:10` displays badge `Tests: 97 Passed`, which is fabricated/stale; the suite actually has 269 tests, and 6 tests in `test/screens/main_screen_test.dart` fail due to obsolete sidebar expectations.

- **[03] R-35 (Test Suite Hygiene)**

### [MEDIUM] Purpose-Gate (Technique Allowed, Purpose Required)

- **[04] R-01 & R-13 (Dead AI Border Beam Widget & Neon Glow)**  
  `lib/widgets/animated_gradient_border.dart` implements a full Google Stitch / Gemini AI light beam with heavy glow and gradient presets (`geminiAurora`, `googleRainbow`), but is dead code never used anywhere in the application UI.

- **[05] R-13 & R-19 (Unused Decorative Pulse Indicators)**  
  `lib/widgets/radar_pulse_indicator.dart` defines `BreathingAmberBadge` and `CyanPulseDot` with infinite glow loops that are completely unused across the app UI.

- **[06] R-08 & R-13 (Run Button Decorative Glow & Default Arrow)**  
  `lib/screens/nexus_screen_command_pane.dart:575-611` applies a heavy neon glow (`blurRadius: 10`, alpha 0.45) and a default arrow icon (`Icons.arrow_forward_rounded`) to the broadcast run button without explicit hierarchy justification.

- **[07] R-04 (Documentation Decorative Emoji Overload)**  
  `README.md:14-80` prefixes almost every section header and feature bullet with generic decorative emojis (`📌`, `✨`, `🖥️`, `📁`, `⚡`, `📓`, `🎨`, `🏗️`, `🚀`), which reads as template AI-generated copywriting.
